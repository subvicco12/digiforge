## Functional scope

`DesignFamilyLibrary` compiles a REVIEW_REQUIRED recipe and typed customer schema against an exact VALIDATED Printify template. Nineteen concept families support local names/initials/monograms, family/couple names, dates, milestones, memorial photos and preserved handwriting. The painterly PT04 concept fails closed: its illustrative preview cannot stand in for an approved PET_PORTRAIT transformation. Recipes are draft compositions for owner review; they do not claim pixel parity with static previews or final artistic approval.

All eight repeat layouts use the actual supplied 16 source positions, uniformly fitted into an explicitly reviewed safe-zone square within each supplier panel. One to eight separately reviewed face fields can alternate; couple/multi-face families require at least two. Explicit tile dimensions, bounded spacing and exact 0/90/180/270-degree lossless rotations are supported. FR03 retains hearts; FR07 retains ribbons and optional per-face customer names; FR06 requires an actual reviewed CS06 monochrome result. Optional names never fall back to sample names. Every placement is checked against safe zones, effective resolution and explicit seam exclusions. Unsupported clipping, seam collisions, rotations or controls are rejected. This supports the same family across separately bound sock, pajama or legging panels; it does not infer anatomical panel relationships, seam continuity or supplier dimensions.

`CustomerCutoutRepository` retains order/shop-scoped source PNG, manually supplied alpha mask and deterministic styled result. Rights, consent and retention references must identify actual protected evidence with matching current hashes. CS01 clean cutout, CS02 white outline, CS03 explicit-color outline, CS04 bounded soft shadow, CS05 circular frame and CS06 grayscale use actual pixels. SOURCE_PHOTO preserves original supported PNG bytes for photo/handwriting families. No learned background-removal model is configured. The follow-on offline selected GrabCut path is documented in `face-cutout-automation.md`; supplied-mask ingestion remains available. Automatic missing-head/halo/artistic checks are not fabricated: an authorized human must explicitly review the listed flags. Empty/clipped masks, unsupported encodings, oversized work, insufficient padding, unreviewed assets or changed evidence fail closed.

Ingestion and review have immutable proofs and audit records. Exact idempotent replays preserve their receipt. A different provenance cannot silently rebind the same already-reviewed order/artifact bytes; reuse the reviewed receipt or provide new artwork. Every render revalidates actual stored bytes, rights evidence and the approval audit. Private paths and image content are not logged. Synthetic evidence remains explicitly SYNTHETIC_TEST_ONLY with commercial/supplier certification false.

`PersonalizedDesignWorkflow` registers authenticated POST routes under `/digiforge/v1`: `/pod/design-family/draft`, `/pod/customer-artwork`, `/pod/customer-artwork/upload`, and `/pod/customer-artwork/review`. Draft compilation reads the exact stored template, checks its shop owner and reproduces its bindings from current persisted variant bytes. Upload accepts canonical bounded base64 PNG source/mask bytes; ingestion accepts existing protected references, rejects other shops and remote/traversal references, and removes temporary staging files. Only authorized POD operators can stage and review customer artwork. These are operator intake boundaries, not anonymous buyer upload endpoints. WordPress REST authentication/CSRF protections remain in force.

Generated recipes use the existing `DeterministicPersonalizationRenderer`, `DeterministicRenderWorkflow`, retained artifact verifier and local SVG/PNG/PDF adapters. Actual approved font glyph data, typed payloads, current supplier bindings and immutable recipe approval are required. Customer text uses Unicode character bounds and actual glyph advances with bounded alignment boxes. Preview and print artifacts are reconstructed from the same canonical approved personalization state; no alternate preview recipe exists. Geometry drift, altered artwork/personalization, cross-shop access, approval replay and mismatched previews are rejected.

## Source mapping and limits

`personalized-design-family-support.tsv` joins actual V2 IDs/source DG IDs from the existing Master 500 index to candidate family recipes for compatible local engines. Candidate means composition reuse, not completed source-specific design acceptance. Face/photo socks DG2-001–020, leggings DG2-361–370 and face-repeat pajamas DG2-371–384 reuse FR families; DG2-385 memorial pajamas use a separately reviewed memorial recipe. Occasion-specific graphics, unsupported transformations and other compositions are not certified by engine compatibility. The original 500-record index still describes every concept, including engines outside this follow-on scope.

Renderer support, draft template availability, authoritative supplier compatibility and commercial certification remain separate. None of the supplied ZIPs establishes Printify blueprint/provider/variant/placement data, bleed/safe-zone/seam/DPI specifications, approved panel continuity, licensed customer photos/fonts, two independent artwork reviewers or a physical supplier sample. Source prototype canvases (560 or 1000 pixels) are never print dimensions. Local raster adaptation retains its existing 1,048,576-pixel capability bound; larger supplier canvases require a separately verified rendering capability and fail closed rather than being resized.
