## Template rules preserved from the source

1. Supplier + product + provider route are selected before production geometry is locked.
2. Provider blueprint/variant print geometry is authoritative; downloadable templates are secondary.
3. Regional physical templates may differ while sharing a personalization engine.
4. Supplier/product/provider changes trigger geometry, bleed, safe-zone, variant and mockup revalidation.
5. Socks, leggings and pajamas require seam/panel-aware rendering and sample QA.
6. Phone-case template identity includes device model/variant.
7. Provider-native placement is preferred for simple placement; DigiForge deterministic rendering handles complex layouts; AI is used only when transformation adds value.
8. Current Printify 40oz blueprint 1509 is not an approved US template route.
9. A strong opportunity is not rejected solely because the first supplier is poor; alternatives are researched.
10. Production authorization freezes template version and render inputs.
