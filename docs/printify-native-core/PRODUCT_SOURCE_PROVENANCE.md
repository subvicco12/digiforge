# Protected product producer and immutable lineage

`PrintifyProductPipeline::receiptSource` inspects successful, actually attempted core product receipts. It requires the signed current session, exact original authorization and request, consumed claim and nonce, exactly two ordered producer audit anchors with the same real actor, current exact policy and provider connection, and current protected workflow/package/artwork records. It does not call a provider, read credential material or issue a grant. STOP/lock remain active for external execution while authenticated local evidence inspection remains available.

`NativePlanEvidence::receipt` consumes this evidence and rechecks the source, shop and session after the expensive protected plan join. Output includes actual immutable workflow, package, artwork, design and commercial-source references. Those hashes are not numeric native product/order versions. Historical product authorizations contain a policy hash but no original revision; `original_approval_revision` remains null. Adopted product-read receipts have a different producer chain and are denied by this dispatch-source method until qualified separately.

Native allocation, business-counter, operational grant, independent revocation/checkpoint and credential-custody authority remain absent. No allocation quantity, epoch or approved business transition is inferred from order display data, observation revisions, plan hashes or these provenance references. Existing observation journal transactions, CAS, replay fencing and UNKNOWN terminal behavior are unchanged.

Focused test: `NativeProductSourceEvidenceTest` creates a synthetic approved package through the real isolated workflow and uses a fake upload response. Inspection must not send HTTP. It checks claim/auth/nonce/producer substitution, missing producer, cross-shop access, lost capability and policy revocation, while preserving false authority flags. Test results and the full composed-artifact gate belong in exact-head receipts, not this specification.
