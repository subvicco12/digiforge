## 2. Mission and finish line
Deliver a usable **DigiCraftifyGoods-only personalized POD factory in the existing DigiForge portal**, supporting:
**shop-scoped opportunity evidence → competitive design brief → three distinct original concepts → approved editable artwork → exact Printify provider template → native personalization plan → mockup evidence → Etsy SEO and conditional price/margin → immutable reviewable listing package → controlled provider-native Etsy handoff**.
A fully automatic image-generation service or autonomous publish is **not** required to satisfy this MVP: where credentials/capabilities are absent, a clearly labeled manual/import step is acceptable and must be demonstrated, not disguised as automation.
No rebuild, new storefront, or full 500-product catalog rollout.

## 4. Scope and acceptance criteria
**P0 in four days**
A. Portal: shop-scoped DigiCraftifyGoods workflow with persisted opportunity, design brief, candidate concepts, evidence/source dates, product/version, and approval state. No wp-admin-only substitute.
B. Research: human-triggered, evidence-backed opportunity/competitor/seasonal signals; distinguish observed demand signals from unverified sales/keyword volumes; three differentiated, original commercial concepts with rationale. If live research API absent, allow curated source import with provenance; mark external automation unavailable.
C. Design: generate/import original editable artwork; separate fixed graphic from native text/photo layers; provider-specific dimensions, 300-DPI check, safe-area status, font/licensing review, and mockup review. No claim that SVG preview alone proves printed output.
D. Product: exact provider/blueprint/variant/SKU where available, native text/image field schema and constraints, manual-review/overflow behavior, mockup rights, order hold and Choice routing evidence.
E. Listing: approved title, 13 relevant Etsy tags, description, category/attributes, five+ relevant mockups where available, seller fee/shipping/ads/FX sensitivity, and configurable *manual* price decision. Never assert modeled contribution is realized profit.
F. Controlled handoff: immutable review package, owner-approved export/Printify-native transfer path, reconciliation of Etsy listing ID/state and shipping/processing effects. **No auto-publish**.
G. Tests: cross-shop isolation, denial on missing approvals/evidence, immutable versions, negative/security checks, focused WordPress integration tests and exact-head Engineering & Safety Audit. Independently inspect the combined candidate before merging.

**Deferred**: 500 listings, bulk automated publication, daily/weekly scheduled AI research, full GST/Finance automation, video factory, 25-provider support, multi-shop expansion, real-order production acceptance, paid automation.

## 7. Non-negotiable safety and economics
- STOP ALL ON, external safety lock ON, automation OFF and RELEASE HOLD unless a separate, narrowly scoped owner authorization explicitly permits an operation. Existing permission to merge audited engineering PRs does not authorize production deploy, live Etsy activation, orders or shop-setting changes.
- Preserve personalized-order Manual approval; no automatic fulfillment, credentials in prompts/logs, or uncontrolled Printify Choice routing.
- Live Etsy buyer preview, provider print-output parity, destination/processing promises, tax/FX/settlement charges remain explicit acceptance gates before a purchasable listing.
- A Printify hidden transfer may create an Etsy **Draft** (observed for FL-02), not necessarily Inactive. Publishing can create/update shared shipping profiles. Do not infer no charges, no customer exposure, or no shared-profile effects without verification.
- Proposed prices and 20% organic margin target are planning policies, not certified net margins. Full GST system is deferred; preserve source financial records and seek qualified tax advice for cross-border treatment.
